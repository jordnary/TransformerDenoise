# 基于窗口 Transformer 的手写数字去噪

## 1. 观测模型与重建准则

### 1.1 灰度归一化与高斯噪声

将灰度像素值除以 $255$，得到干净图像 $X\in[0,1]^{H\times W}$。以 $X$ 为重建目标，含噪观测满足

$$
Y=X+\varepsilon,\qquad
\varepsilon_{ij}\overset{\mathrm{i.i.d.}}{\sim}\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2),\qquad
\sigma=\frac{50}{255}\approx0.196078.
$$

训练、验证和测试采用相同的噪声分布，含噪输入保留超出 $[0,1]$ 的像素值。

### 1.2 $L_1$ 重建损失与输出范围

网络以残差形式估计干净图像：

$$
F_{\theta}(Y)=Y+R_{\theta}(Y).
$$

设训练样本数为 $N$，唯一的重建损失为像素平均绝对误差，即 $L_1$ 损失：

$$
\mathcal{L}_{1}(\theta)
=\frac{1}{NHW}\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sum_{i=1}^{H}\sum_{j=1}^{W}
\left|F_{\theta}(Y_n)_{ij}-X_{n,ij}\right|.
$$

推理采用一次前向计算，并对重建结果进行范围投影：

$$
\widehat{X}=\Pi_{[0,1]}\!\left(F_{\theta}(Y)\right),\qquad
\Pi_{[0,1]}(z)=\min\{1,\max\{0,z\}\}.
$$

该投影逐像素作用于网络输出。对 $x\in[0,1]$，有 $|\Pi_{[0,1]}(z)-x|\leq|z-x|$，因此不会增加相对于干净图像的 $L_1$ 误差或均方误差。

## 2. Transformer 模型：自注意力、位置编码与 SwiGLU

### 2.1 图像块表示与两种输入尺寸的适配

输入在右侧和下侧反射填充至 $8$ 的倍数，再在四周各反射扩展 $2$ 个像素，以步长 $2$ 提取 $6\times6$ 重叠图像块。每个图像块展平为 $36$ 维向量，经线性层映射为 $64$ 维特征。输出端为每个标记预测对应的 $2\times2$ 残差块，按空间顺序重排后与含噪图像相加。

设适配后的尺寸为 $H_p\times W_p$，则

$$
H_p=8\left\lceil\frac{H}{8}\right\rceil,\qquad
W_p=8\left\lceil\frac{W}{8}\right\rceil.
$$

两种输入尺寸共用同一组网络参数，重建后裁回原始尺寸，整个过程不缩放图像。

表 1：输入尺寸、标记网格与输出尺寸。

| 输入尺寸 | 适配后的尺寸 | 标记网格 | 输出尺寸 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $28\times28$ | $32\times32$ | $16\times16$ | $28\times28$ |
| $32\times32$ | $32\times32$ | $16\times16$ | $32\times32$ |

### 2.2 多头自注意力（Self-Attention）

每个窗口包含 $4\times4$ 个标记。设窗口特征矩阵为 $Z\in\mathbb{R}^{16\times64}$，第 $a$ 个注意力头的计算为

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_a&=ZW_a^{Q}+\mathbf{1}b_a^{Q},\\
K_a&=ZW_a^{K}+\mathbf{1}b_a^{K},\\
V_a&=ZW_a^{V}+\mathbf{1}b_a^{V},\\
\operatorname{Attn}_a(Z)
&=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(
\frac{Q_aK_a^{\mathsf{T}}}{\sqrt{d_h}}+B_a+M
\right)V_a,
\qquad d_h=16.
\end{aligned}
$$

其中，$a\in\{1,2,3,4\}$，$W_a^Q,W_a^K,W_a^V\in\mathbb{R}^{64\times16}$，$b_a^Q,b_a^K,b_a^V\in\mathbb{R}^{1\times16}$，$\mathbf{1}\in\mathbb{R}^{16\times1}$；$\operatorname{softmax}$ 沿键的位置维度计算。四个头的输出拼接后经线性映射融合，得到 $16\times64$ 的窗口特征。$B_a$ 为位置偏置，$M$ 为窗口掩码。

### 2.3 位置编码：二维相对位置偏置

空间位置信息通过注意力得分中的可学习偏置 $B_a$ 表示。若标记 $i$ 与 $j$ 的坐标差为 $(\Delta r_{ij},\Delta c_{ij})$，则

$$
(B_a)_{ij}
=\rho_a\!\left[7(\Delta r_{ij}+3)+(\Delta c_{ij}+3)\right],
\qquad
\Delta r_{ij},\Delta c_{ij}\in\{-3,-2,\ldots,3\}.
$$

其中，$\rho_a\in\mathbb{R}^{49}$，索引从 $0$ 开始。每个注意力头具有 $49$ 个相对位置参数，其索引仅依赖窗口内的坐标差，不依赖整幅图像的尺寸。

网络共包含 $8$ 个 Transformer 块，交替采用普通窗口和沿两个空间方向平移 $2$ 个标记的窗口，以交换相邻窗口的信息。移位窗口中，掩码对循环平移产生的不相邻连接赋值 $-10^{4}$，其余位置取 $0$。

### 2.4 SwiGLU 前馈映射与残差连接

每个块采用预归一化结构。记相应的普通或移位窗口注意力映射为 $\mathcal{A}_{\ell}$，则

$$
\begin{aligned}
U_{\ell}&=Z_{\ell}+\mathcal{A}_{\ell}\!\left(\operatorname{LN}(Z_{\ell})\right),\\
Z_{\ell+1}&=U_{\ell}+\operatorname{SwiGLU}\!\left(\operatorname{LN}(U_{\ell})\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

其中，$\operatorname{LN}$ 表示层归一化，SwiGLU 前馈映射为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{SwiGLU}(z)
&=\left[\operatorname{SiLU}(zW_1+b_1)\odot(zW_2+b_2)\right]W_o+b_o,\\
\operatorname{SiLU}(u)&=u\,\operatorname{sigmoid}(u).
\end{aligned}
$$

两条分支分别扩展至 $128$ 维，经逐元素门控后映射回 $64$ 维。最后一个 Transformer 块后接层归一化与残差重建线性层。重建层零初始化，使网络初始映射为恒等映射。模型不含卷积层，可训练参数共 338,660 个。

## 3. MNIST 数据划分与训练设置

MNIST 的 60,000 张 $28\times28$ 训练图像按随机种子 3407 划分为 55,000 张训练图像和 5,000 张验证图像。训练时对干净图像进行直角旋转与镜像增强，再生成独立高斯噪声；验证噪声种子固定为 12345。数字标签不输入网络，全部参数从零训练，不调用现成去噪模型，不使用外部训练图像或预训练权重。

训练分为三个阶段。基础阶段训练 40 轮，按验证 $L_1$ 误差选择第 40 轮的指数移动平均（EMA）权重；随后进行 12 轮灰度增强训练。灰度增强以 $0.5$ 的概率保持图像不变，其余情况采用

$$
\begin{aligned}
X'&=b+a\,\mathcal{T}(X),\\
a&\sim\mathcal{U}(0.25,1),\qquad
b\mid a\sim\mathcal{U}(0,1-a),\\
\mathbb{P}\!\left(\mathcal{T}(X)=X\right)
&=\mathbb{P}\!\left(\mathcal{T}(X)=1-X\right)=\frac{1}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

增强后的目标仍位于 $[0,1]$，含噪输入为 $X'+\varepsilon$。该阶段使用原灰度验证图像和固定种子 6789 生成的灰度增强验证图像，以两个验证域的平均 $L_1$ 误差等权选取检查点：

$$
S(\theta)=\frac{1}{2}\left(
\mathcal{L}_{1,\mathrm{original}}(\theta)
+\mathcal{L}_{1,\mathrm{augmented}}(\theta)
\right).
$$

选定第 12 轮的 EMA 权重，其未作范围投影的验证结果如下。

表 2：灰度增强阶段所选权重的验证结果。

| 验证域 | $L_1$ | 平均 PSNR / dB |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 原灰度 | 0.018265 | 25.723 |
| 灰度增强 | 0.034176 | 24.559 |

对应选择指标为 $S=0.026221$。最后以该检查点初始化，在全部 60,000 张训练图像上固定优化 8 轮，采用第 8 轮 EMA 权重，不再使用原验证集选优。各阶段均使用 AdamW 优化器和批大小 256。

$$
\bar{\theta}_{t}
=\beta_t\bar{\theta}_{t-1}+(1-\beta_t)\theta_t,
\qquad
\beta_t=\min\!\left\{0.995,\frac{1+t}{10+t}\right\}.
$$

训练在支持 CUDA 的 GPU 上完成，并采用自动混合精度；推理使用 FP32，误差统计使用 FP64。

## 4. 数字 0-9 的去噪图像与逐图 PSNR

### 4.1 PSNR 定义与测试集总体结果

对官方测试集的 10,000 张图像独立评估。第 $n$ 张图像的均方误差及峰值信噪比定义为

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{MSE}_n
&=\frac{1}{HW}\sum_{i=1}^{H}\sum_{j=1}^{W}
\left(\widehat{X}_{n,ij}-X_{n,ij}\right)^2,\\
\operatorname{PSNR}_n
&=10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{1}{\operatorname{MSE}_n}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

PSNR 的峰值固定为 $1$，单位为 dB。设测试图像数为 $N_{\mathrm{test}}$，平均 PSNR 按逐图值计算：

$$
\overline{\operatorname{PSNR}}
=\frac{1}{N_{\mathrm{test}}}\sum_{n=1}^{N_{\mathrm{test}}}\operatorname{PSNR}_n.
$$

主测试噪声种子为 20261029，各方法使用相同的含噪输入。高斯平滑基线采用固定的 $3\times3$ 核、$0.7$ 像素的空间标准差和反射边界。表中两种重建方法均将输出投影至 $[0,1]$。

表 4：MNIST 官方测试集的总体去噪结果。

| 方法 | $L_1$ | MSE | 平均 PSNR / dB |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 含噪输入 | 0.156429 | 0.038433 | 14.158 |
| 高斯平滑 | 0.060310 | 0.009790 | 20.158 |
| Transformer | 0.016963 | 0.002771 | 25.804 |

Transformer 的平均 PSNR 为 $25.804\,\mathrm{dB}$，相对含噪输入提高 $11.646\,\mathrm{dB}$，相对高斯平滑提高 $5.647\,\mathrm{dB}$。逐图 PSNR 的样本标准差为 $1.475\,\mathrm{dB}$；由平均 MSE 换算的 PSNR 为 $25.574\,\mathrm{dB}$，与逐图 PSNR 的算术平均不同。

测试噪声的经验均值为 $0.000072$、标准差为 $0.196045$。含噪像素范围为 $[-1.131,1.874]$，与未经裁剪的高斯加噪设置一致。

## 6. 讨论

模型通过重叠图像块、普通与移位窗口注意力及二维相对位置偏置，在无卷积结构下完成像素级残差重建。灰度增强覆盖不同背景亮度、对比度和灰度反转，最终采用单次前向计算与范围投影。MNIST 测试结果表明，该方案能够有效抑制指定强度的高斯噪声，并保留主要数字笔画。

$L_1$ 训练目标与基于均方误差的 PSNR 并不完全一致，重建结果仍可能损失细节。给定图像的结果显示模型对自然纹理的适应性有限；对同一图像重复加噪只能反映噪声变化的影响，不能据此推断对其他自然图像的泛化性能。

### A.2 Transformer 去噪模型：`denoise/model.py`

```python
"""A residual Transformer with linear patch projections and shifted windows."""

import torch
from torch import nn
from torch.nn import functional as F


def partition(x, window):
    b, h, w, c = x.shape
    return x.reshape(b, h // window, window, w // window, window, c).permute(
        0, 1, 3, 2, 4, 5).reshape(-1, window * window, c)


def reverse(x, window, h, w):
    return x.reshape(-1, h // window, w // window, window, window, x.shape[-1]).permute(
        0, 1, 3, 2, 4, 5).reshape(-1, h, w, x.shape[-1])


def shift_mask(h, w, window, shift, device):
    labels = torch.zeros((1, h, w, 1), device=device)
    slices = (slice(0, -window), slice(-window, -shift), slice(-shift, None))
    for i, ys in enumerate(slices):
        for j, xs in enumerate(slices):
            labels[:, ys, xs] = i * 3 + j
    labels = partition(labels, window).squeeze(-1)
    return (labels.unsqueeze(1) != labels.unsqueeze(2)).float() * -1e4


class Attention(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, dim, heads, window):
        super().__init__()
        self.heads, self.window = heads, window
        self.qkv = nn.Linear(dim, dim * 3)
        self.proj = nn.Linear(dim, dim)
        self.bias = nn.Parameter(torch.zeros((2 * window - 1) ** 2, heads))
        coords = torch.stack(torch.meshgrid(torch.arange(window), torch.arange(window), indexing="ij"))
        delta = coords.flatten(1)[:, :, None] - coords.flatten(1)[:, None, :]
        delta = delta + window - 1
        self.register_buffer("index", delta[0] * (2 * window - 1) + delta[1])
        nn.init.trunc_normal_(self.bias, std=0.02)

    def forward(self, x, mask):
        b, n, c = x.shape
        q, k, v = self.qkv(x).reshape(b, n, 3, self.heads, c // self.heads).permute(2, 0, 3, 1, 4).unbind(0)
        bias = self.bias[self.index].permute(2, 0, 1).to(q.dtype)
        if mask is not None:
            windows = mask.shape[0]
            bias = bias[None] + mask[:, None].to(q.dtype)
            bias = bias.repeat(b // windows, 1, 1, 1)
        y = F.scaled_dot_product_attention(q, k, v, attn_mask=bias, dropout_p=0.0)
        return self.proj(y.transpose(1, 2).reshape(b, n, c))


class SwiGLU(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, dim):
        super().__init__()
        self.up = nn.Linear(dim, 4 * dim)
        self.down = nn.Linear(2 * dim, dim)

    def forward(self, x):
        a, b = self.up(x).chunk(2, dim=-1)
        return self.down(F.silu(a) * b)


class Block(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, dim, heads, window, shift):
        super().__init__()
        self.window, self.shift = window, shift
        self.norm1, self.norm2 = nn.LayerNorm(dim), nn.LayerNorm(dim)
        self.attn, self.ffn = Attention(dim, heads, window), SwiGLU(dim)

    def forward(self, x, mask):
        h, w = x.shape[1:3]
        z = self.norm1(x)
        if self.shift:
            z = torch.roll(z, (-self.shift, -self.shift), (1, 2))
        z = reverse(self.attn(partition(z, self.window), mask if self.shift else None), self.window, h, w)
        if self.shift:
            z = torch.roll(z, (self.shift, self.shift), (1, 2))
        x = x + z
        return x + self.ffn(self.norm2(x))


class Denoiser(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, dim=64, depth=8, heads=4, patch=2, window=4, halo=0, output_halo=0):
        super().__init__()
        if dim < 1 or heads < 1 or dim % heads or patch < 1 or window < 2 or depth < 1 or min(halo, output_halo) < 0:
            raise ValueError("Invalid Transformer dimensions")
        self.patch, self.window, self.halo = patch, window, halo
        self.output_halo = output_halo
        self.embed = nn.Linear((patch + 2 * halo) ** 2, dim)
        self.blocks = nn.ModuleList(Block(dim, heads, window, (i % 2) * (window // 2)) for i in range(depth))
        self.norm, self.head = nn.LayerNorm(dim), nn.Linear(dim, patch * patch)
        nn.init.zeros_(self.head.weight)
        nn.init.zeros_(self.head.bias)
        if output_halo:
            self.refine = nn.Linear(2 * dim, (patch + 2 * output_halo) ** 2)
            nn.init.zeros_(self.refine.weight)
            nn.init.zeros_(self.refine.bias)

    def initialize(self, weights):
        """Allow only a zero-initialized refinement branch to be added."""
        missing = set(self.state_dict()) - set(weights)
        if missing not in (set(), {"refine.weight", "refine.bias"}):
            raise ValueError("Initialization architecture mismatch")
        self.load_state_dict({**self.state_dict(), **weights}, strict=True)

    def forward(self, x):
        if x.ndim != 4 or x.shape[1] != 1 or min(x.shape[-2:]) < 8:
            raise ValueError("Expected grayscale BCHW images with both dimensions >= 8")
        b, _, h, w = x.shape
        p, unit = self.patch, self.patch * self.window
        pad_h, pad_w = (-h) % unit, (-w) % unit
        noisy = F.pad(x, (0, pad_w, 0, pad_h), mode="reflect")
        hh, ww = noisy.shape[-2] // p, noisy.shape[-1] // p
        kernel = p + 2 * self.halo
        z = F.pad(noisy, (self.halo,) * 4, mode="reflect") if self.halo else noisy
        z = z.unfold(2, kernel, p).unfold(3, kernel, p).permute(0, 2, 3, 1, 4, 5)
        z = z.reshape(b, hh, ww, kernel * kernel)
        z = shallow = self.embed(z)
        mask = shift_mask(hh, ww, self.window, self.window // 2, x.device)
        for block in self.blocks:
            z = block(z, mask)
        z = self.norm(z)
        residual = self.head(z).reshape(b, hh, ww, p, p).permute(0, 1, 3, 2, 4)
        residual = residual.reshape(b, 1, hh * p, ww * p)
        if self.output_halo:
            k = p + 2 * self.output_halo
            patches = self.refine(torch.cat((z, shallow), dim=-1)).flatten(1, 2).transpose(1, 2)
            options = dict(output_size=(hh * p, ww * p), kernel_size=k, padding=self.output_halo, stride=p)
            divisor = F.fold(torch.ones_like(patches[:1, :, :]), **options)
            residual = residual + F.fold(patches, **options) / divisor
        return (noisy + residual)[..., :h, :w]
```

```python
"""Data, deterministic noise, metrics, and inference shared by the commands."""

import hashlib
import json
import random
from pathlib import Path

import numpy as np
import torch
from torch.nn import functional as F
from torchvision.datasets import MNIST

SIGMA = 50 / 255


def resolve_device(requested="auto"):
    if requested not in ("auto", "cpu", "cuda"):
        raise ValueError("Device must be auto, cpu, or cuda")
    if requested == "cuda" and not torch.cuda.is_available():
        raise RuntimeError("CUDA is unavailable; use --device cpu or --device auto")
    return torch.device("cuda" if requested == "auto" and torch.cuda.is_available() else "cpu" if requested == "auto" else requested)


def setup(seed, threads=4):
    random.seed(seed)
    np.random.seed(seed)
    torch.manual_seed(seed)
    torch.cuda.manual_seed_all(seed)
    torch.set_num_threads(threads)
    torch.backends.cudnn.benchmark = False
    torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32 = False


def read_json(path):
    return json.loads(Path(path).read_text(encoding="utf-8"))


def write_json(path, value):
    path = Path(path)
    path.parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    temporary = path.with_suffix(path.suffix + ".tmp")
    temporary.write_text(json.dumps(value, ensure_ascii=False, indent=2, allow_nan=False) + "\n", encoding="utf-8")
    temporary.replace(path)


def save_checkpoint(path, value):
    path = Path(path)
    temporary = path.with_suffix(path.suffix + ".tmp")
    torch.save(value, temporary)
    temporary.replace(path)


def dataset(root, train):
    ds = MNIST(root, train=train, download=True)
    return ds.data[:, None], ds.targets


def split_indices(size, validation_size, seed):
    order = torch.randperm(size, generator=torch.Generator().manual_seed(seed))
    return order[validation_size:], order[:validation_size]


def add_noise(clean, generator=None):
    return clean + SIGMA * torch.randn(clean.shape, device=clean.device, generator=generator)


def tone_augment(clean, probability, generator):
    draws = torch.rand((4, len(clean), 1, 1, 1), device=clean.device, generator=generator)
    contrast = 0.25 + 0.75 * draws[0]
    base = torch.where(draws[1] < 0.5, 1 - clean, clean)
    transformed = contrast * base + (1 - contrast) * draws[2]
    return torch.where(draws[3] < probability, transformed, clean)


def structure_augment(clean, probability, generator):
    """Affine crops and convex mixtures using only the current clean batch."""
    if not 0 <= probability <= 1:
        raise ValueError("Augmentation probability must be in [0, 1]")
    if probability == 0:
        return clean
    n = len(clean)
    draws = torch.rand((2 * n, 4), device=clean.device, generator=generator)
    angle, scale = draws[:, 0] * (2 * torch.pi), 0.7 + 1.3 * draws[:, 1]
    theta = clean.new_zeros((2 * n, 2, 3))
    theta[:, 0, 0] = theta[:, 1, 1] = angle.cos() * scale
    theta[:, 0, 1], theta[:, 1, 0] = -angle.sin() * scale, angle.sin() * scale
    theta[:, :, 2] = (draws[:, 2:] * 2 - 1) * 0.65
    source = torch.cat((clean, clean.flip(0)))
    grid = F.affine_grid(theta, source.shape, align_corners=False)
    first, second = F.grid_sample(source, grid, align_corners=False).chunk(2)
    choices = torch.rand((3, n, 1, 1, 1), device=clean.device, generator=generator)
    weight = 0.25 + 0.5 * choices[0]
    mixed = weight * first + (1 - weight) * second
    transformed = torch.where(choices[1] < 0.5, mixed, first)
    return torch.where(choices[2] < probability, transformed, clean)


def structure_validation(images, seed):
    generator = torch.Generator().manual_seed(seed)
    clean = F.pad(images.float() / 255, (2,) * 4)
    return tone_augment(structure_augment(clean, 1, generator), 1, generator)


def fixed_noise(shape, seed):
    return SIGMA * torch.randn(shape, generator=torch.Generator().manual_seed(seed))


def scores(pred, clean):
    error = (pred.double() - clean.double()).flatten(1)
    mse = error.square().mean(1)
    return {"l1": error.abs().mean(1), "mse": mse, "psnr": -10 * torch.log10(mse)}


def summarize(metrics):
    result = {key: float(value.mean()) for key, value in metrics.items()}
    psnr = metrics["psnr"]
    result["psnr_std"] = float(psnr.std(unbiased=len(psnr) > 1))
    result["psnr_standard_error"] = result["psnr_std"] / len(psnr) ** 0.5
    result["psnr_from_mse"] = float(-10 * torch.log10(metrics["mse"].mean()))
    return result


def first_indices(labels):
    return torch.tensor([int(torch.where(labels == digit)[0][0]) for digit in range(10)])


@torch.inference_mode()
def predict(model, noisy, ensemble=False):
    if not ensemble:
        return model(noisy).float()
    total = torch.zeros_like(noisy)
    for flip in (False, True):
        for k in range(4):
            z = noisy.flip(-1) if flip else noisy
            y = model(z.rot90(k, (-2, -1))).float().rot90(-k, (-2, -1))
            total += y.flip(-1) if flip else y
    return total / 8


def sha256(path):
    return hashlib.sha256(Path(path).read_bytes()).hexdigest()
```

```python
"""Train with fresh Gaussian corruption and validation-only model selection."""

import argparse
import copy
import math
import time
from pathlib import Path

import torch
from torch.nn import functional as F

from .common import add_noise, dataset, fixed_noise, read_json, resolve_device, save_checkpoint, setup, sha256, split_indices, structure_augment, structure_validation, tone_augment, write_json
from .model import Denoiser


@torch.inference_mode()
def validate(model, images, noise, batch_size, device):
    model.eval()
    errors = torch.zeros(3, device=device, dtype=torch.float64)
    for start in range(0, len(images), batch_size):
        clean = images[start:start + batch_size].to(device).float()
        clean = clean if images.is_floating_point() else clean / 255
        noisy = clean + noise[start:start + batch_size].to(device)
        pred = model(noisy).float()
        diff = (pred - clean).double().flatten(1)
        mse = diff.square().mean(1)
        errors += torch.stack((diff.abs().mean(1).sum(), mse.sum(), (-10 * mse.log10()).sum()))
    return dict(zip(("l1", "mse", "psnr"), (errors / len(images)).tolist()))


def run(config, output, full=False, resume=None, device_name="auto", initialize=None):
    cfg = read_json(config)
    setup(cfg["seed"])
    device = resolve_device(device_name)
    output = Path(output)
    output.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    if (output / "last.pt").exists() and resume is None:
        raise ValueError("Run already exists; choose another output or explicitly resume")
    all_images, _ = dataset(cfg["data_root"], True)
    train_ids, val_ids = split_indices(len(all_images), cfg["validation_size"], cfg["split_seed"])
    images = all_images if full else all_images[train_ids]
    validation = None if full else all_images[val_ids]
    noise = None if full else fixed_noise(validation.shape, cfg["validation_noise_seed"])
    tone_validation = None
    if not full and cfg.get("tone_probability", 0):
        tone_validation = tone_augment(validation.float() / 255, 1, torch.Generator().manual_seed(cfg["tone_seed"]))
    structural = structural_noise = None
    if not full and cfg.get("structure_seed") is not None:
        structural = structure_validation(validation, cfg["structure_seed"])
        structural_noise = fixed_noise(structural.shape, cfg["validation_noise_seed"])
    images = images.to(device)
    model = Denoiser(**cfg["model"]).to(device)
    initialization = None
    if initialize:
        if resume:
            raise ValueError("Initialization requires a new run")
        selected = torch.load(initialize, map_location=device, weights_only=True)
        if not full and selected["full"]:
            raise ValueError("Full-data weights would contaminate the validation split")
        if not full and any(selected["config"][key] != cfg[key] for key in ("split_seed", "validation_size", "data_root")):
            raise ValueError("Initialization must preserve the validation split")
        source_arch, target_arch = selected["config"]["model"].copy(), cfg["model"].copy()
        source_halo, target_halo = source_arch.pop("output_halo", 0), target_arch.pop("output_halo", 0)
        if source_arch != target_arch or source_halo not in (0, target_halo):
            raise ValueError("Initialization architecture mismatch")
        model.initialize(selected["model"])
        initialization = sha256(initialize)
    ema = copy.deepcopy(model).eval().requires_grad_(False)
    optimizer = torch.optim.AdamW(model.parameters(), lr=cfg["lr"], weight_decay=cfg["weight_decay"])
    amp = device.type == "cuda" and cfg["amp"]
    amp_dtype = torch.bfloat16 if device.type == "cuda" and torch.cuda.is_bf16_supported() else torch.float16
    scaler = torch.amp.GradScaler(device.type, enabled=amp and amp_dtype == torch.float16)
    history, best, start_epoch, step = [], float("inf"), 0, 0
    if resume:
        state = torch.load(resume, map_location=device, weights_only=False)
        if state["config"] != cfg or state["full"] != full:
            raise ValueError("Resume configuration differs from the saved run")
        model.load_state_dict(state["model"])
        ema.load_state_dict(state["ema"])
        optimizer.load_state_dict(state["optimizer"])
        scaler.load_state_dict(state["scaler"])
        history, best, start_epoch, step = state["history"], state["best"], state["epoch"], state["step"]
        initialization = state.get("initialization_sha256")
    write_json(output / "config.json", cfg)
    write_json(output / "protocol.json", {"full": full, "train_count": len(images), "validation_count": 0 if full else len(validation), "parameters": sum(p.numel() for p in model.parameters()), "initialization_sha256": initialization, "selection": "fixed final epoch" if full else "minimum mean L1 over enabled fixed validation domains", "train_indices": None if full else train_ids.tolist(), "validation_indices": None if full else val_ids.tolist()})
    batches = math.ceil(len(images) / cfg["batch_size"])
    total_steps, warmup = cfg["epochs"] * batches, cfg["warmup_epochs"] * batches
    for epoch in range(start_epoch, cfg["epochs"]):
        begin = time.perf_counter()
        generator = torch.Generator(device=device).manual_seed(cfg["seed"] + epoch * 1009)
        order = torch.randperm(len(images), generator=generator, device=device)
        model.train()
        total_loss = 0.0
        for ids in order.split(cfg["batch_size"]):
            clean = images[ids].float() / 255
            if cfg["augment"]:
                code = int(torch.randint(8, (), generator=generator, device=device))
                clean = clean.rot90(code % 4, (-2, -1))
                clean = clean.flip(-1) if code >= 4 else clean
            if cfg.get("mixed_sizes", False) and bool(torch.randint(2, (), generator=generator, device=device)):
                clean = F.pad(clean, (2,) * 4)
            if cfg.get("structure_probability", 0):
                clean = structure_augment(clean, cfg["structure_probability"], generator)
            if cfg.get("tone_probability", 0):
                clean = tone_augment(clean, cfg["tone_probability"], generator)
            noisy = add_noise(clean, generator)
            step += 1
            ratio = step / max(1, warmup) if step <= warmup else cfg["min_lr_ratio"] + (1 - cfg["min_lr_ratio"]) * (1 + math.cos(math.pi * (step - warmup) / max(1, total_steps - warmup))) / 2
            for group in optimizer.param_groups:
                group["lr"] = cfg["lr"] * ratio
            optimizer.zero_grad(set_to_none=True)
            with torch.autocast(device.type, dtype=amp_dtype, enabled=amp):
                pred = model(noisy)
                loss = F.l1_loss(pred.float(), clean)
            if not torch.isfinite(loss):
                raise FloatingPointError("Non-finite training loss")
            scaler.scale(loss).backward()
            scaler.unscale_(optimizer)
            torch.nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_(model.parameters(), cfg["grad_clip"], error_if_nonfinite=True)
            scaler.step(optimizer)
            scaler.update()
            decay = min(cfg["ema_decay"], (1 + step) / (10 + step))
            with torch.no_grad():
                for average, parameter in zip(ema.parameters(), model.parameters()):
                    average.lerp_(parameter, 1 - decay)
            total_loss += float(loss.detach()) * len(ids)
        row = {"epoch": epoch + 1, "train_l1": total_loss / len(images), "lr": optimizer.param_groups[0]["lr"]}
        if not full:
            row.update({"val_" + key: value for key, value in validate(ema, validation, noise, cfg["eval_batch_size"], device).items()})
            if tone_validation is not None:
                row.update({"tone_" + key: value for key, value in validate(ema, tone_validation, noise, cfg["eval_batch_size"], device).items()})
            if structural is not None:
                row.update({"structure_" + key: value for key, value in validate(ema, structural, structural_noise, cfg["eval_batch_size"], device).items()})
            domains = [row[key] for key in ("val_l1", "tone_l1", "structure_l1") if key in row]
            row["selection_l1"] = sum(domains) / len(domains)
        row["seconds"] = round(time.perf_counter() - begin, 3)
        history.append(row)
        improved = full or row["selection_l1"] < best
        best = best if full else min(best, row["selection_l1"])
        payload = {"config": cfg, "model": ema.state_dict(), "epoch": epoch + 1, "full": full, "initialization_sha256": initialization}
        if improved:
            save_checkpoint(output / "model.pt", payload)
        save_checkpoint(output / "last.pt", {**payload, "model": model.state_dict(), "ema": ema.state_dict(), "optimizer": optimizer.state_dict(), "scaler": scaler.state_dict(), "history": history, "best": best, "step": step})
        write_json(output / "history.json", history)
        print(" ".join(f"{k}={v:.6f}" if isinstance(v, float) else f"{k}={v}" for k, v in row.items()), flush=True)
    return history


if __name__ == "__main__":
    parser = argparse.ArgumentParser(description=__doc__)
    parser.add_argument("--config", default="configs/overlap.json")
    parser.add_argument("--output", default="runs/selection")
    parser.add_argument("--full", action="store_true", help="Use all training images, with a fixed epoch budget")

    args = parser.parse_args()
    run(args.config, args.output, args.full, args.resume, args.device, args.initialize)
```

```python
def gaussian_baseline(x):
    axis = torch.arange(-1, 2, device=x.device, dtype=x.dtype)
    weights = torch.exp(-axis.square() / (2 * 0.7 ** 2))
    kernel = weights[:, None] * weights[None, :]
    kernel = kernel / kernel.sum()
    patches = F.pad(x, (1, 1, 1, 1), mode="reflect").unfold(2, 3, 1).unfold(3, 3, 1)
    return (patches * kernel).sum((-1, -2))

        noisy = x + noise[start:start + batch].to(device)
        values = {"noisy": noisy, "gaussian": gaussian_baseline(noisy), "single": predict(model, noisy)}
        if ensemble:
            values["ensemble"] = predict(model, noisy, True)
        for name, value in values.items():
            predictions[name].append(value.cpu())

    for name in ("gaussian", "single", "ensemble"):
```

### A.6 结果绘图：`denoise/figures.py`

```python
"""Publication figures drawn from saved, unmodified floating-point arrays."""

from pathlib import Path

import matplotlib
matplotlib.use("Agg")
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import numpy as np

from .common import read_json


def comparison(arrays, method, path, columns, labels):
    fig, axes = plt.subplots(3, len(columns), figsize=(max(7, len(columns) * 1.9), 5.9), squeeze=False)
    for row, name in enumerate(("clean", "noisy", method)):
        for col, index in enumerate(columns):
            ax = axes[row, col]
            image = arrays[name][index, 0]
            ax.imshow(image, cmap="gray", vmin=0, vmax=1, interpolation="nearest")
            ax.set_xticks([])
            ax.set_yticks([])
            for spine in ax.spines.values():
                spine.set_visible(False)
            if row == 0:
                ax.set_title(labels[col], fontsize=11)
            else:
                mse = np.mean((image.astype(np.float64) - arrays["clean"][index, 0]) ** 2)
                ax.set_title(f"PSNR {(-10 * np.log10(mse)):.2f} dB", fontsize=10)
            if col == 0:
                ax.set_ylabel(("Clean", "Noisy", "Denoised")[row], fontsize=11)
    fig.tight_layout(pad=1.0)
    fig.savefig(path, dpi=200, bbox_inches="tight", facecolor="white")
    plt.close(fig)


def render_results(output, method):
    output = Path(output)
    with np.load(output / "digits.npz") as arrays:
        for start in (0, 5):
            comparison(arrays, method, output / f"digits_{start}_{start + 4}.png", range(start, start + 5),
                       [f"Digit {i} | index {arrays['indices'][i]}" for i in range(start, start + 5)])
    if (output / "external.npz").exists():
        with np.load(output / "external.npz") as arrays:
            fig, axes = plt.subplots(1, 3, figsize=(9, 3.5))
            h, w = arrays["clean"].shape[-2:]
            for ax, key, title in zip(axes, ("clean", "noisy", method), (f"Clean ({h} x {w})", "Noisy", "Denoised")):
                ax.imshow(arrays[key][0, 0], cmap="gray", vmin=0, vmax=1, interpolation="nearest")
                if key != "clean":
                    mse = np.mean((arrays[key].astype(np.float64) - arrays["clean"]) ** 2)
                    title += f"\nPSNR {(-10 * np.log10(mse)):.2f} dB"
                ax.set_title(title)
                ax.axis("off")
            fig.tight_layout()
            fig.savefig(output / "external.png", dpi=220, bbox_inches="tight", facecolor="white")
            plt.close(fig)


def learning_curve(history_path, output):
    history = read_json(history_path)
    epochs = [row["epoch"] for row in history]
    fig, axes = plt.subplots(1, 2, figsize=(10, 3.7))
    axes[0].plot(epochs, [row["train_l1"] for row in history], label="Training")
    axes[0].plot(epochs, [row["val_l1"] for row in history], label="Validation (EMA)")
    axes[0].set(xlabel="Epoch", ylabel="L1 loss")
    axes[0].legend(frameon=False)
    axes[1].plot(epochs, [row["val_psnr"] for row in history], color="#167d9a")
    axes[1].set(xlabel="Epoch", ylabel="Validation PSNR (dB)")
    for ax in axes:
        ax.grid(alpha=0.2)
    fig.tight_layout()
    fig.savefig(output, dpi=200, bbox_inches="tight", facecolor="white")
    plt.close(fig)
```
